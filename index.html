A little about myself

I'm a junior transfer student at UCLA, originally from a small town outside of Boston, Massachusetts. My passion for history and statistics comes from a deep fascination with wanting to better understand the world around me — so I can eventually make a meaningful and positive contribution to the planet.

Growing up near Boston gave me an appreciation for the stories that data can tell about the past and present. I believe that numbers, when understood in their historical and social context, are one of the most powerful tools we have for creating positive change.

Projects

01

The Marriage Penalty: A Census Income Analysis

Investigated whether marriage is a financial penalty for men using 1994 US Census data. Built an interactive heatmap visualization showing income rates by marital status and education level. Findings challenged the common narrative — married men consistently out-earn never-married men across nearly every education level.

Pokémon Data Analysis

Exploratory data analysis of Pokémon statistics using R. Analyzed patterns in base stats, types, and generations to uncover trends across the Pokémon universe. Created visualizations showcasing distributions and correlations between key attributes.
